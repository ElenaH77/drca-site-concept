March 8, 2023 DRCA Membership Meeting Notes 7:00 p.m. Board introductions and welcome  7:05 p.m. Crime and traffic report by Captain Dennis Andreas 

  * Aggravated assaults (2) in Del Ray and an arrest was made in both cases
  * Burglaries (2) at the smoke shop
  * 18 larcenies over 30 days in Del Ray including 4 shoplifting, package theft, stolen license plates, 7 catalytic converters (Hondas and a Toyota). They recommend you paint the catalytic converters with a special heat paint. 
  * A stolen auto
  * 20 new officers on the Alexandria Police force
  * The body camera program will roll out this spring with 30 cameras

7:10 p.m.: Sheriff's Office report by Sheriff Sean Casey

  * On their way to reaccreditation receiving top scores by the accrediting agency (triple crown, only 50 in the country with this status)
  * They are looking for volunteers at the detention center. You can [sign up here. ](https://www.alexandriava.gov/sheriff/volunteer-at-the-sheriffs-office)

7:15 p.m.: Congressman Don Beyer for an Update and Q&A

  * Unfortunately the Congressman was not able to attend

Presentation to Alexandria Celebrates Women

  * The chili cook-off was a huge success! We had over 300 people and raised $2700 for the Alexandria Celebrates Women event. 
  * ACW will donate all of the funds to the domestic violence shelter in Alexandria. If you would like to [donate follow the link here. ](https://alexandriacelebrateswomen.com/)

Announcements

  * DRCA Gardenfest will be on Sunday, April 23 from 12-4pm at 2207 Mount Vernon Avenue. There will be many vendors including a greenhouse with Paw Paw seedlings. 
  * DogFest is Sunday, April 2 from 11am-3pm and will have 37 different vendors. [https://www.visitdelray.com/events/del-ray-dog-fest-2023/](https://www.visitdelray.com/events/del-ray-dog-fest-2023/)
  * Community Lodgings is in need of diapers and cleaning supplies. Please bring items to the farmer’s market this Saturday. 
  * Del Ray Artisans have a new exhibit and ongoing classes. [Check it out!](https://delrayartisans.org/exhibits/) They will have extended hours on First Thursdays. 
  * Parks committee - they are still waiting to hear back from the City on approval for the Adopt-a-park program for this coming year. We typically start up again in April.
  * MVPS PTA update with Elizabeth Reyna.
    * The Big Flea market is not happening this year due to storage issues. The was a large fundraiser this year
    * Upcoming science night with Cora Kelly
    * Soccer Saturday hosted by ASA
    * English classes for Spanish speaking parents at the school
    * Teacher grant funding up to $400 a year
    * Fundraisers for dental, lice treatment, field trips, and scholarships

8:00 p.m.: Adjourn
