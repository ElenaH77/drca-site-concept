  * **DRCA Bylaws Committee**
    * DRCA Secretary, committee chair, Sean Crumley provided a short introduction to the Bylaws Committee work to date
    * DRCA President Jay Nestelrode discussed some possible changes to DRCA officer descriptions meant to attract new talent and recruit new board members.
    * Current direction: 
      * 1VP (Parliamentarian and Presumptive Next President)
      * 2VP-->VP Programs, coordinating DRCA events and meeting programming
      * 3VP-->VP Membership, chairing membership and outreach
      * Chief Information Officer in lieu of Sergeant-at-Arms.
  * **La Bella Strada**
    * Candie Ellis present a funding request for $500 for La Bella Strada event, scheduled for May 14 (rain date 5/15), organized to benefit the Del Ray Montessori School. A number of neighborhood business and groups have already pledged sponsorship.
    * One member expressed concern about setting precedent, whether this was a one-time request or whether the event might be annual.
    * Amy Slack moved to approve $500 pending approvals of needed permits. The motion was seconded by Jon Quandt and was carried with no nays, no abstentions
  * **Land Use Committee, Lisa Quandt**
    * Discussion of proposal to move membership meeting to third week
    * This would allow LUC recommendations to be presented first to Board then to Members then to Planning Commission.
    * Intent: Allow thought thoughtful Board and Member input to be incorporated before DRCA presents to Planning Commission.
    * Member concerns: Conflict with Thanksgiving and Christmas holidays
    * Vote in March
