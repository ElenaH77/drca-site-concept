title: "DRCA committee to consider proposal for traffic calming, bike lanes on Monroe Avenue"
date: "2013-09-21"
categories: "Pedestrian Safety, Transportation"
type: "post"
---
The DRCA [Transportation and Pedestrian Safety Committee ](http://delraycitizen.wordpress.com/committees/traffic-parking/ "Transportation / Pedestrian Safety")will consider a city staff proposal for traffic calming and bicycle lanes on East Monroe Ave. between Mount Vernon and Leslie avenues. For further information, contact Committee chair Jay Nestlerode at [jnestlerode@gmail.com](mailto:jnestlerode@gmail.com).
