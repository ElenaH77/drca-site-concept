The Del Ray Citizens Association's Sixth Annual Chili Cook-off was held on February 9, 2019 and raised **$2333** for the construction of a [new park](https://www.gofundme.com/newpark) at the corner of E. Bellefonte Ave and Route 1. We could not have raised such a sum without the incredible generosity of the winners in each category, as well as the winner of the 50/50 raffle, who all donated back the prize money. 

The winners were: 

_Meat:_ Christi Hart and Daniela Spigai (tie) 

_Vegetarian:_ Sue Kovalsky 

_People's Choice:_ Kristine Hesse 

_50/50:_ Daniela Spigai 

Prizes were sponsored once again by Bobi Bomar and Ellen Patrick. Thank you, Bobi and Ellen!
