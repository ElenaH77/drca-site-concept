[![2014-02-Reincarnations](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/02/2014-02-reincarnations.jpg?w=204)](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/02/2014-02-reincarnations.jpg)“Reincarnations,” the current [Del Ray Artisans](http://www.thedelrayartisans.org/) show, continues until March 2 at the group’s gallery at the Colasanto Center, 2704 Mount Vernon Ave. “Reincarnations” shows how artists have a way of rethinking, repurposing, refurbishing, recycling, and recreating in ways that can lead to unexpected expressions and alternative effects on the environment.
