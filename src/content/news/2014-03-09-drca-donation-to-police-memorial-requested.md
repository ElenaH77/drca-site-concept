---
title: "DRCA donation to police memorial requested"
date: "2014-03-09"
categories: "Funding"
type: "post"
---
Representatives of the Alexandria Police Foundation have requested that the DRCA consider a $1,000 contribution to a Fallen Officers Memorial to Alexandria police officers who have died in the line of duty. We will consider the request at our meeting tomorrow. Contributors of at least $1,000 will have their names inscribed on the giving wall of the memorial, which is to be located at the police department headquarters on Wheeler Avenue. For further information, visit their [website](http://www.alexandriapolicefoundation.org/).
