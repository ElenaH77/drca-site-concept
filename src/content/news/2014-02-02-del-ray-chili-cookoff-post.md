Windsor Ave. If you would like to enter the competition, please visit the [registration page](https://delraycitizens.org/meetings/chili-cook-off/chili-cook-off-registration/ "Chili Cookoff Registration"). The entry fee is $25, with the money being applied to current or future DRCA membership dues. Cash prizes are being awarded. See [rules page](https://delraycitizens.org/meetings/chili-cook-off/ "Chili Cookoff") for more details. The cookoff sponsor is Bobi Bomar’s [Homes of Alexandria](http://www.homesofalexandria.com/). All DRCA members are invited to attend the event, even if you don’t plan to cook. We suggest that you bring cornbread or dessert, but that is optional. For more information, contact DRCA first vice president [Jay Nestlerode](mailto:jnestlerode@gmail.com).
