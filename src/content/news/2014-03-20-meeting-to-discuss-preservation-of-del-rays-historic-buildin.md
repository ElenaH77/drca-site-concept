By Bill Hendrickson, DRCA president The city is beginning a process that will hopefully lead to greater recognition of more of Del Ray's historic buildings as well as increased efforts to preserve them. This effort is especially aimed at preserving Del Ray's many Art Deco and [Streamline Moderne](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streamline_Moderne) structures. A kickoff meeting will be held on Monday, March 24, at 7 p.m. at the [Mount Vernon Recreation Center](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Mount+Vernon+Recreation+Center/@38.8286187,-77.0601298,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0xb3d853a024a49d02). Neighborhood support is vital in this effort, so please try to attend. If you can't attend, but are interested in following this effort, please email me at [whendrick@aol.com](mailto:whendrick@aol.com).
