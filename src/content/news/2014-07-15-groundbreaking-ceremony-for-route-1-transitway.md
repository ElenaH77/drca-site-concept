[![Metroway_Aug_2014-01](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/07/metroway_aug_2014-01.png?w=195)](https://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/07/metroway_aug_2014-01.png)A groundbreaking ceremony for the region's first dedicated bus transitway will take place Friday, July 18, 9:00 am at 33rd Street and Crystal Drive in Arlington. The transitway is described as a mechanism for providing faster, more reliable bus service along the congested Route 1 corridor. The bus route will connect the Braddock Road Metro Station to the Crystal City Metro Station. More details about the event can be [found here](http://projects.arlingtonva.us/events/crystal-city-potomac-yard-transitway-groundbreaking/), or general information about the system from the [City of Alexandria transitway page](https://www.alexandriava.gov/tes/info/default.aspx?id=58644).
