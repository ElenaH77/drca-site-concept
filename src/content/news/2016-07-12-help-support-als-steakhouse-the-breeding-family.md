As most everyone in town is aware, **Al's Steakhouse** suffered a devastating fire on July 6, 2016 the same day as their grand re-opening. Please help support this neighborhood establishment by donating to the Breeding family in any way you can. Holy Cow is hosting an [Al's Pop Up Restaurant](http://www.holycowdelray.com/als-steakhouse-fundraiser) on Thursday July 14th at 5:30, [FireFlies](http://www.firefliesdelray.com/) is collecting donations inside their restaurant, and a [Go Fund Me](https://www.gofundme.com/2dpzu24?utm_source=internal&utm_medium=email&utm_content=campaign_title&utm_campaign=donation_receiptv5) page has been set up to raise $5,000. Read more about the fire in the [Del Ray Patch](http://patch.com/virginia/delray/holy-cow-host-als-steak-house-pop-fundraiser) article. DRCA has donated to the Breeding family, and we hope our members will provide support as well.![als-steakhouse-fundraiser](https://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2016/07/als-steakhouse-fundraiser.jpg)
