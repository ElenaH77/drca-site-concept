The next meeting of the Duncan Library Poetry Society will feature Del Ray resident Jodie Hollander reading from her poetry collection, The Humane Society. The reading is tomorrow, March 10, at 8 p.m. in the [Duncan Library](http://www.alexandria.lib.va.us/client/en_US/home/?rm=DUNCAN0%7C%7C%7C1%7C%7C%7C1%7C%7C%7Ctrue&dt=list) meeting room. The event is free.
