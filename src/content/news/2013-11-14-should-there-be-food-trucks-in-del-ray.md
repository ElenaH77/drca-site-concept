![DC_food_truck](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2013/11/dc_food_truck.jpg)The city’s Food Truck Policy Advisory Group will meet on Monday, November 18, at 6 p.m. at City Hall in the City Council workroom to solicit comments on the feasibility of food trucks in Del Ray. Residents, business owners, area workers, and members of the public are invited to attend this meeting and express their opinions. If you are unable to attend the meeting, comments can be submitted to Rose Boyd, special assistant to the city manager, at [rose.boyd@alexandriava.gov](mailto:rose.boyd@alexandriava.gov). For more information, go to [www.alexandriava.gov/FoodTrucks](http://www.alexandriava.gov/FoodTrucks). [polldaddy poll=7563525]
