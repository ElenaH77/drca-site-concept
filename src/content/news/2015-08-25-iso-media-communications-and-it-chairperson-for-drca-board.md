The Del Ray Citizens Association is seeking out a Media Communications and IT Chairperson to sit on the board of directors for the 2015-2016 season. 

**Duties include:**

  * keeping the website up-to-date,
  * communicating events and information via twitter and Facebook (in addition to the website) on a regular basis,
  * meet with and interact with key local government officials,
  * and actively attend monthly membership meetings and monthly board meetings.

The ideal candidate will have experience in media communication (twitter and Facebook), knowledge of website design, and willingness to revamp website in an effort to make it user friendly.

This is a volunteer position. If interested, please email [jnestlerode@gmail.com](mailto:jnestlerode@gmail.com).
