The Del Ray Citizens Association hosts a House and Garden Tour every even numbered year. Proceeds from the tour are used to fund a $1,000 per year donation to The Scholarship Fund of Alexandria. Proceeds beyond the scholarship donation go towards beautification of the neighborhood and public art. The 2020 House and Garden Tour is planned for Saturday, May 9, 2020 from 11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. rain or shine. If you would like to have your home considered for the tour or if you have a suggestion of a home or garden you would like to see featured, please fill out the form at <https://delraycitizens.org/committees/house-and-garden-tour/>.
