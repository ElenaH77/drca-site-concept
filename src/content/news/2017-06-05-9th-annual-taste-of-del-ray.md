# _SELL OUT CROWD AT TASTE OF DEL RAY_

  Not only was there a sold-out crowd of 1,000 hungry patrons, but a record 18 restaurants entered the 9th Annual Taste of Del Ray to feature their salivary seductions. The line started to form shortly after 12pm to buy the remaining tickets for the Taste of Del Ray and by the time guests were allowed through the gates, it was a sell out. At the height of the event, lines crisscrossed the parking lot of [United Bank](https://www.bankwithunited.com/) as hungry guests waited to try the culinary creations of the Avenue restaurants. The strategy of the day that proved successful was to handoff the tasty treats to your line partner, very similar to a baton race where your gustatory cells were the ultimate winner. Across the street [Mars Rodeo](http://marsrodeo.com/) (not Radio), although they seem to enjoy the frequent mispronunciation, kicked off the music portion of the edible event. While wandering through the thick crowds, Swing's Coffee was getting a lot of mention with their Bourbon Barrel Coffee. The[ Alexandria Boys and Girls Club ](http://www.bgcgw.org/clubs/dunbar-alexandria-olympic-branch/)raised over $1,000 from drink sales and donations! If you weren't able to attend this year, don't worry there are only 364 days left until the 10th Annual Taste of Del Ray and I'm sure the [Del Ray Business Association](http://www.visitdelray.com/) is already plotting the course for the 10 year anniversary of this amazing event!
