Alexandria City Councilman [Justin Wilson](http://www.justin.net/) will speak at our membership meeting on Monday, December 9, on the city’s new effort to explore options for expanded broadband television and Internet service in Alexandria; in short, alternatives to Comcast. State Delegate [Rob Krupicka](http://www.krupicka.com/) will also attend to discuss the key issues that the General Assembly is expected to focus on in the upcoming session. The meeting begins at 7:30 p.m. at the Reingold office building, 433 E.
