The DRCA was excited to host Senator Adam Ebbin and Delegate Elizabeth Bennett-Parker for a virtual town hall to discuss the Potomac Yard Arena proposal. Mayor Justin Wilson and Stephanie Landrum from the Alexandria Economic Development Partnership also joined the meeting to share additional details and context. You can watch the recording of the session here:  [embed]https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCO5mjcB11M[/embed] AEDP also shared a number of new slides with additional context about the project - those slides are available here:  <https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Monumental_final-01.08.23.pdf> We have also made all citizen-submitted questions available to city officials, and you can [view them here.](https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Arena-Town-Hall-with-Senator-Ebbin-and-Delegate-Bennett-Parker_Submitted-Questions-.pdf)
