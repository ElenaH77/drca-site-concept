**Thank You Alexandria first responde rs for attending our June end-of-year party ** On Sunday June 25, first responders to the tragic Simpson Stadium shooting on June 14 from the Alexandria Police, Fire and Sheriffs Departments attended our social event. President Rod Kuckro gave a toast in their honor, and food and drink was enjoyed by all. The executive board for 2017-2018 was unanimously approved. Thank you to everyone at the No. 9 Lounge for hosting such a wonderful event.  Enjoy the summer and we look forward to seeing you at our next membership meeting in September.
