**DRCA Membership Meeting, December 14, 2022** **Agenda:** 7:00 p.m.: Business meeting begins promptly  7:05 p.m.: Crime and traffic report by Captain Dennis Andreas

  * Crime is slightly up including a couple of larcenies from cars, shoplifting, burglary case on E. Luray linked to other cases in the city
  * 22 traffic stops, focusing on DUI enforcement
  * SoberRide.com for a free ride home

7:15 p.m.: Sheriff's office report by Daniel Gordon

  * Recently attended CALEA (Committee and accreditation of law enforcement agencies) conference in St. Louis.
  * [Operation Elf](https://www.alexandriava.gov/sheriff/sheriffs-office-welcomes-donations-for-operation-elf) open right now. Donations are being accepted for families of jail “residents”.
  * The Sheriff’s office is handing out Meals on Wheels on December 22 for Alexandria Senior Services.
  * Home checks are available during the holidays. There is an[ online form linked here](https://www.alexandriava.gov/sheriff/away-from-home-check)
  * The 2023 Alexandria Sheriff’s community class is available in March.
