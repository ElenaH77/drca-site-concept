At our April 2024 membership meeting, the DRCA hosted Alexandria Mayoral Candidates Amy Jackson, Steven Peterson, and Alyia Gaskins for a candidate forum. The candidates were each given the [same set of questions submitted by DRCA members](https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Mayoral-Forum-Candidate-Instructions-and-Questions.pdf) and asked to speak for 10 minutes uninterrupted in response to those questions. The order of the speakers was chosen at random.   [embed]https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=atjAN4WXp4A[/embed]
