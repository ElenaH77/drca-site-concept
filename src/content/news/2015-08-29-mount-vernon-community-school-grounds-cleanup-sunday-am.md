Join your Del Ray neighbors for a spruce- up of the Mount Vernon Community School grounds and gardens this Sunday, August 30th, from 8:00-12:00pm.

Keeping this space clean and attractive is of great value to all in the neighborhood. Sunday's volunteer day is a great way to be part of the action, contribute to our community, and learn more about plans to renovate the play space.

Donuts and coffee will be served. Children are welcome!

This event is organized by the Playground Committee at MVCS
