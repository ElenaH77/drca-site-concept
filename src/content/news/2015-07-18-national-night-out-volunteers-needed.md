We have started planning for another NNO this August 4th and need many volunteers to support this event to ensure its success. The event will be held at the Mount Vernon Req Center Athletic Fields again this year August 4th from 5:30pm to 7:30pm. We need volunteers to help the day of the event to help setup, manage food and cleanup after the event. If you are interested in volunteering this year please email Jay Nestlerode (president@delraycitizen.net) directly.
