      * District 6, 11 already exist
      * 2 week study
      * Weekdays and weekend
      * Turnover counts (included farmers market lot)
      * Peaking at 77% whole area 
        * Just MVA Peak 86%
        * Side street : 74%
        * Raymond to Bellefonte: Peak at 97%

      * Recommendations 
        * Shared parking: Big opportunity
        * Extend 2hour limits to 9PM, not 5
        * Residential Permit expand or create new zones 
          * Petition process, driven by residents
        * Meters for farmers market
        * Wayfinding aids to existing off-street availability
        * Carshare expansion
        * 18 bus stops along MVA that need ADA compliance 
          * Leverage opportunities for better crossings, reduce number of bus stops, use bump-outs to save parking (old style bus stop take away more spots)
      * Many questions 
        * Price for multiple vehicle permits
        * Enforcement needed (!!)
        * Why private lots underused?
        * Households with multiple cars an problem
        * Meters will push parking to side streets making problem worse
        * Enforcement
        * Need list of SUP parking exceptions (problem with Variety Store)
        * Problem with parking a sign of Del Ray's success
