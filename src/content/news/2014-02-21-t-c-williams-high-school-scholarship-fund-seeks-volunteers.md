The Scholarship Fund of T.C. Williams High School is seeking 100 volunteers to read the applications of students applying for need-based and merit scholarships. This reading event will take place on Saturday, March 8, from 8 a.m. to noon, but volunteers can arrive whenever is convenient and stay as long as you can. Coffee and bagels will be served. Please sign up at the [following site](http://www.signupgenius.com/index.cfm?go=t.SignUpMemberManagement&listid=2424114&memberid=11625242.).
