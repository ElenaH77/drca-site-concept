The city is going to begin work on the interim park improvements to Mt. Jefferson Park,  starting with tree removal at the future Stewart/Randolph entrance to the park. For more information, see below: 

> Site work is proceeding to open up the new Stewart Avenue/Randolph Avenue entrance to Mt.Jefferson Park/WOD trail. Tree removal work is scheduled to begin next week, weather permitting. The pathway work is anticipated to be complete late July. Tree plantings will be scheduled this fall. For additional information about the interim park improvements in this area of Mt. Jefferson Park, please click the link below. <http://www.alexandriava.gov/recreation/info/default.aspx?id=85382>
