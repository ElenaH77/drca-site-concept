---
title: "City mulch delivery is now available"
date: "2014-03-09"
categories: "Beautification"
type: "post"
---
Residents can begin placing orders for delivery of mulch beginning tomorrow, March 10. Delivery dates and times are limited, and appointments are on a first-come, first-served basis. The mulch is free, but there is a $40 delivery charge for a half or a full truckload. Mulch can also be picked up for free from 7 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday through Saturday, at 4215 Eisenhower Ave. For more information visit: [www.alexandriava.gov/YardWaste](www.alexandriava.gov/YardWaste).
