---
title: "City to hold kickoff meeting on possible Del Ray historic preservation plan"
date: "2014-03-17"
categories: "Beautification"
type: "post"
---
The city’s Department of Planning and Zoning will host a kickoff meeting to discuss two upcoming projects in Del Ray, a nonbinding residential pattern book and a survey of the Art Deco and Streamline Moderne buildings, on Monday, March 24, at 7 p.m. at the Mount Vernon Recreation Center, 2701 Commonwealth Ave. The two projects are aimed at developing a possible Del Ray historic preservation plan. The city agreed to develop the plan after the big turnout at the November 2012 DRCA-sponsored Del Ray Historic Preservation Conference, which highlighted the need to find ways to preserve Del Ray’s many Art Deco and Streamline Moderne buildings. For questions or comments, contact Stephanie Sample or Al Cox of the planning department at 703-746-3833 or at preservation@alexandriava.gov.
