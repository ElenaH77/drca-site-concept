![duncan](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/03/duncan-e1395026856944.jpg)The [Friends of Duncan Library](http://www.friendsofduncanlibrary.org/) is hosting a social hour on Tuesday, March 18, 7 p.m. to exchange ideas for improving the library and talk about volunteer opportunities. The event will be held at the home of Carolyn and Jim Harris, 2907 Cameron Mills Road. You do not need to be a Friends member to attend. For further information or to RSVP, email [duncanlibraryfriends@gmail.com](mailto:duncanlibraryfriends@gmail.com).
