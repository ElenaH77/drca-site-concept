  * **Land Use Committee Report,** Barry Nolan 
    * Revisit Sicilian Pizza proposal
    * Business used to be in the Calvert. Parking sufficient for 4 tables 8 chairs. Overlay zone a few issues (business wants to use backlit sign from previous location)
    * Applicant: Not a chain but a local business. Workers suffering from loss of business. Never hit a person with delivery. Working with Walgreens for parking their vans.
    * LUC voted 6-1 against SUP: Business out of character for neighborhood. Concerns for safetly and need for future customer parking. Rooftop equipment an eyesore.
    * Amendment by Amy Slack to put conditions (business hours , screening rooftop equipment ): 
      * Should Planning Commission recommend supporting SUP, we ask for midnight operating hours, screening equipment, apply for 15-minute parking spots
      * Amendment carries unanimously
    * Motion from committee: Approve LUC refusal to support SUP 
      * Original motion: Pro 9, against 18, 5 abstentions.
      * Marlin moves to support SUP with the Slack amendment (conditions)
    * Motion carries: 26, 5, 0
  * **Alexandria Police** , Capt. Dennis Andreas
