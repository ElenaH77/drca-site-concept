Saturday, May 2nd 2015,1:00 to 4:00 p.m. (Rain date: Sunday, May 3rd 2015, 1:00 to 4:00 p.m.) There will be a garden work party on the grounds of the Monroe Ave. YMCA in Alexandria (across the street from the Giant and CVS). These gardens have been installed and are maintained by volunteers. Work parties are usually held in the spring and fall, but we can always use help, so please contact us if you are interested. Here is your chance to pitch in, meet your neighbors, and help beautify Del Ray. Help is needed in a variety of activities, including weeding, replacing bamboo edgings, and mulching, so bring your favorite tools* and meet us in the back parking lot, near the north door of the YMCA building. We look forward to seeing you! *If you don’t have tools we’ll have some extras. Questions? Call Audrey or Bob at 703-549-1367
