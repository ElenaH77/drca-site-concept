April 9, 2025 – Held virtually via Zoom. DRCA received an update from the ACPS Redistricting Advisory Committee (RAC). The City provided an update on the Commonwealth, Glebe, Ashby Flood Mitigation Project and Volunteer Alexandria provided an update on emergency preparedness. The Land Use Committee briefed the membership on the 2525 Mount Vernon Ave Proposal and the membership voted.
