The DRCA Land Use Committee is seeking new members. Volunteers are invited to attend the committee meeting on Thursday, December 12, at 7 p. m. at the Mount Vernon Recreation Center to learn more about what the committee does. See full meeting agenda below. In general, the committee discusses and takes positions on land use and development proposals that must be approved by the Planning Commission and City Council. The committee invites applicants, city staff, potentially affected residents, and other interested parties to its meetings, held on the second Thursday of each month. If you are interested in joining the committee but can’t attend Thursday’s meeting, please contact co-chairs [Lisa Quandt](mailto:lisamaq@gmail.com) or [Danielle Fidler](mailto:dcfidler1@hotmail.com). **Land Use Committee Monthly Meeting/ Open House Agenda** **December 12, 2013 - 7:00PM** **Mount Vernon Recreation Center** 1\.
