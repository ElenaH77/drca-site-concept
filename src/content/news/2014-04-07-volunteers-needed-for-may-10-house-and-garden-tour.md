Volunteers are needed for the 2014 Del Ray House & Garden Tour on Saturday, May 10. Volunteers will check tickets at the homes and monitor the visitors. Two-hour shifts are available from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m., from 1 to 3 and 3 to 5 p.m. Each volunteer will receive a free ticket to the tour and an invitation to the post-tour party. If you would like to volunteer, email [Chas Harris](mailto:chasharris@comcast.net). Please include your phone number. You will receive an acknowledgment email and shifts will be assigned by the middle of April.
