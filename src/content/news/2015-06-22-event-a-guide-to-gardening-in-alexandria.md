[![Simpson_garden](https://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2015/06/x-y-garden.jpg?w=150)](https://delraycitizens.org/2015/06/22/event-a-guide-to-gardening-in-alexandria/x-y-garden/)The [Alexandria Beautification Commission](http://alexandriava.gov/Beautification) and Virginia Cooperative Extension invite you to learn to plan and tend an urban garden. Whether you're new to the challenges of urban gardening or have specific issues that need addressing, this program is for you! Kirsten Buhls, the Virginia Cooperative Extension agent for Alexandria, will be addressing the challenges of urban gardening and the best ways to overcome them. The program is free and open to the public. Registration is requested -- sign up online at [mgnv.org](http://mgnv.org/). The Best Management Practices for Urban Landscapes: A Guide to Gardening in Alexandria Tuesday June 30, 7:30-9:00 pm Mount Vernon Recreation Center, 2701 Commonwealth Avenue, Alexandria, VA
