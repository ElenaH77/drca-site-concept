May 17, 2023 Membership Meeting Notes Agenda: 7:00 p.m. Business meeting begins promptly -- board introductions and welcome 7:05 p.m. Crime and traffic report by Captain Dennis Andreas

  * Increase in crime city-wide. Reassigning officers to go to the hot spots in the city.
  * Del Ray had 9 larcenies over the past month. Four bikes stolen off from front porches of homes. 
  * 2 robberies, first robbery victim was uncooperative, second robbery victim was intoxicated
  * Last month burglary at Shell station on Mt. Vernon
  * Burglary at 2300 block of Mt. Vernon, known suspect
  * Less traffic enforcement as officers transition to higher crime areas
  * Click-it or ticket month – it is seat belt awareness month
  * Next Thursday, May 25 at 6pm there will be a city community cook-out at the Mt. Vernon field behind the school 
  * Bank robbery suspect apprehended

7:10 p.m. Sheriff's office report by Sheriff Sean Casey (Officer Robyn Nichols)

  * No covid cases in the jail in over a month
  * Cannot have public visitors right now because the elevator is broken
  * Staff busy at community events, hosting 2 cookouts a week
  * Sheriff’s office offers away from home checks while you’re on vacation – link on their website. 

7:15 p.m. Presentation on Simpson Park Renovation

  * Presenter was unable to attend
  * Park and Rec Commission Meeting [information linked here](https://www.alexandriava.gov/news-rpca/2023-04-27/park-recreation-commission-to-hold-a-public-hearing-on-proposed-renovation-to). 
  * Project [website linked here](https://www.alexandriava.gov/parks/project/eugene-simpson-athletic-diamond-fields-renovation-plan). 

7:35 p.m. Presentation on Power of Produce

  * Encouraging kids to attend the farmer’s market and learn about where their food comes from. There will be an enrichment activity and once they participate, they will receive a $5 voucher to purchase something at the market. 

7:45 p.m. Presentation on Scholarship fund of Alexandria

  * They give scholarships and offer college advising – they have two full-time college advisors. 
  * Many of the kids they work with don’t speak English as a first language or live on their own.
  * They will help kids fill out the FAFSA form.
  * This year they will give out 209 scholarships, 75% are needs-based applications. Usually have about 400 kids who apply. 
  * Email address for Michele, Director for The Scholarship Fund of Alexandria. Email her and she can get your name added to the scholarship application read list: michelle.gleeson@acps.k12.va.us

7:55 p.m. LUC presentation on Hops N Shine SUP and vote

  * Requesting additional seating and firepit areas
  * The LUC talked to neighbors and businesses
  * Extending dining hours on weekends
  * Increase music from speakers and amplified live entertainment on patio (trivia, paint night, etc.)

MOTION FOR HOPS N SHINE DRCA SUPPORTS MAINTAINING THE CONDITIONS OF THE CURRENT 2020-00045 SUP  DRCA RECOMMENDS THE FOLLOWING:

  * Work with the Mt. Vernon Court Community Association (MVCCA) to improve the sound barrier between the two properties.
  * Provide plastic mulch or Astroturf on pavement to help absorb sound
  * Eliminate outside Cigar Night event
  * The Applicant demonstrate compliance for 18 months with the conditions of the 2020-0045 SUP
  * Appointment of a community liaison with MVCCA
  * Consider reorienting the patio speakers to mitigate sound traveling
  * Limit speaker use to background music (no live music or entertainment)
