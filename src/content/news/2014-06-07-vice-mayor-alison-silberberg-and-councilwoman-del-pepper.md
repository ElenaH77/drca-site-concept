title: "Vice Mayor Alison Silberberg and Councilwoman Del Pepper to speak on environmental issues at next DRCA meeting"
date: "2014-06-07"
categories: "Member Meetings"
type: "post"
---
Learn more about the city’s environmental initiatives at the monthly meeting of the Del Ray Citizens Association on Monday, June 9, at 7:30 p.m. at the Reingold building, 433 E. Monroe Ave. Alexandria Vice Mayor Alison Silberberg and Councilwoman Del Pepper will speak on environmental sustainability in the city, and Stiles Peabody of the city’s [Department of Environmental Services](http://www.alexandriava.gov/Environment) will speak about the city’s expanding recycling program. Come early at 7 p.m. for refreshments and conversation with your neighbors.
