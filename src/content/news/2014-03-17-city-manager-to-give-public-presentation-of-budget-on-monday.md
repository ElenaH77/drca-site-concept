date: "2014-03-17"

---
City Manager [Rashad Young](http://www.alexandriava.gov/CityManager) will host a public presentation of his proposed fiscal year 2015 budget tomorrow, Monday, March 17, at Beatley Central Library. The event is free and open to anyone who would like to attend. Following his presentation, Young will answer questions. A live stream of the event will also be broadcast on AlexTV (Channel 70) and online at alexandriava.gov/AlexTV. For more information on the proposed budget or to view the entire document, visit [alexandriava.gov/Budget](http://alexandriava.gov/Budget).
