[Alexandria Police Chief Earl Cook](http://www.alexandriava.gov/police/info/default.aspx?id=24438) will speak at the DRCA membership meeting on Monday, April 14. Bill has asked him to talk about some of the larger issues related to police protection and enforcement today. How has the type of crimes committed during the past 10-15 years changed, and how has that changed the composition of the force and its focus? How did 9/11 change the overall focus of the department? To what degree is emergency response a greater concern today? How have police methods evolved over time, especially with the use of new technologies that are also raising privacy concerns? This is certain to be a stimulating discussion.
