[![photo](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/03/photo.jpg?w=300)](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/03/photo.jpg)Valerie Loomer (left) and Tina Chancey perform during a recent concert in the circa-1920s home of Deborah Brautigam and David Hirschmann on Dewitt Avenue. They performed Irish music from the earliest sources on original instruments. Chancey played baroque violin and viol, and Loomer played [theorbo](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theorbo), early guitar, and cittern.
