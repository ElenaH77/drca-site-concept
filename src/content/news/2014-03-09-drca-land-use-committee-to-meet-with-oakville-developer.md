title: "DRCA Land Use Committee to meet with Oakville developer"
date: "2014-03-09"
categories: "Development, Land Use"
type: "post"
---
At its regular monthly meeting, the Land Use Committee will meet on Thursday, March 13, with representatives of the company that is considering redevelopment of about 13 acres of the 20-acre Oakville Triangle industrial park, located on the west side of Route 1 bordering a residential portion of Del Ray. The meeting will be held at 7 p.m. at the Mount Vernon Recreation Center. All are invited. For more information about possible redevelopment at Oakville, see previous [postings](https://delraycitizens.org/2014/02/02/oakville-developer/ "Oakville developer eyes mixed-use project").
