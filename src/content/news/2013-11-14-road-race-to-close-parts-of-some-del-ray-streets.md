date: "2013-11-14"

---
The U.S. National Road Race Championship on Sunday, November 17, will temporarily close some streets in Del Ray. The race begins in Old Town at 7 a.m., and the street closures in Del Ray will occur beginning at about 7:25 a.m. when runners come from Old Town via West Street, turn on to East Braddock Road, then on to Mount Vernon Avenue, and then on to East Monroe Avenue. The complete course map can be [viewed here](http://www.nationalroadracingchampionships.us/Road_Race_Championships/files/f4/f4068011-bf4b-4f11-a13c-6c6f0e288da3.pdf).
