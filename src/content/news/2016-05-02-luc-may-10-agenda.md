The following items are on the Land Use Committee’s agenda for its May 10th meeting at the Mt. Vernon Rec Center at 7 pm. All are welcome to attend and provide comments. The cases will be voted on at the May 11th DRCA membership meeting. **a. 113 E. Custis Ave SUB 2016-003. 7:10 – 7:40 pm.** Subdivision of existing property into two lots to construct two single family residences which will require demolition of contributing structure to the Town of Potomac Historic district. **b. 1504B Mt. Vernon Ave SUP 2016-0030. 7:45 – 8:15 pm.** Special Use Permit for a pizza delivery and carry-out restaurant. Applicant proposes seating for eight and parking available in the back parking lot.
