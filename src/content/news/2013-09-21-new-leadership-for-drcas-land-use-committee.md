Danielle Fidler and Lisa Quandt have taken over as co-chairs of DRCA's [Land Use Committee](http://delraycitizen.wordpress.com/committees/land-use/ "Land Use"). They replace Sarah Haut and Kristen Gedeon. Sarah is stepping down because of another commitment, but will remain on the committee. Kristen has for moved to Baltimore for a job. Thank you very much to both of them for their hard work, dedication and above all, leadership of land use issues affecting Del Ray.
