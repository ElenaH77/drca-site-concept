The DRCA recently invited the Hume Springs Civic Association, the Lynhaven Civic Association, and the Rosemont Citizens Association to join us in an official survey of our membership lists regarding the proposed Arena and Performing Arts Center project in Potomac Yard. On this page we've provided a detailed memo explaining how the survey was conducted and analyzing the findings across all four civic associations. You can also access the full results including all open ended comments, both for Del Ray, and for all four Civic Associations together.
