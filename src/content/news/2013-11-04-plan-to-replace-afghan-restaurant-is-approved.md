title: "Plan to replace Afghan Restaurant is approved"
date: "2013-11-04"
categories: "Development, Land Use"
type: "post"
---
![Afgan](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2013/11/afgan.jpg)At its October 1 meeting, the Planning Commission approved the construction of two small, one-story commercial buildings to replace the Afghan Restaurant on Route 1 between Calvert and East Raymond avenues. The buildings are designed to accommodate between two and seven retail businesses. The developer agreed to pay a fee in lieu of undergrounding utility lines along Route 1. Thanks to the [DRCA Land Use Committee](http://delraycitizen.wordpress.com/committees/land-use/ "Land Use") for its analysis and recommendations on this project, which helped influence a much-improved building design.
