The following item is on the Land Use Committee’s agenda for its June 14th meeting at the Mt. Vernon Rec Center at 7 pm. All are welcome to attend and provide comments. The case will not be voted on at the next DRCA membership meeting as the case will go to the Planning Commission on July 5th. If you have an interest in this case, please attend the LUC meeting. **2619 E. Randolph Ave Amendment to Subdivision 2016-0005.** Application to amend previously approved subdivision. The applicant proposes to leave the existing residence and construct a single family residence on the second lot. The original 2005 subdivision had been approved for demolishing the existing residence and constructing semi-detached residences.
