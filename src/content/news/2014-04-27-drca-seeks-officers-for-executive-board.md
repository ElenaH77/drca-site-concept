Anyone who wishes to become more involved in our great Del Ray neighborhood should consider applying for a position on the DRCA Executive Board. Elections are held in June for a one-year term. To see a list of the specific offices and their responsibilities, please read the DRCA bylaws at [www.delraycitizens.org/about](http://delraycitizens.us7.list-manage.com/track/click?u=dc79560375b6a09d924e12ce7&id=f3f30871a2&e=1b2d156d3f). If you are interested in applying, send an email to Sarah Haut, chair of the Nominations and Election Committee, at [hautsl@yahoo.com](mailto:hautsl@yahoo.com).
