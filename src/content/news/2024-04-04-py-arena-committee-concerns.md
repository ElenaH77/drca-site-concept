After the announcement of the proposed arena and entertainment complex at Potomac Yard, the DRCA stood up a committee of interested members and citizens to study the issue and meet regularly to discuss neighborhood concerns as well as potential remedies and solutions in preparation for an eventual consideration phase in Alexandria. Although that phase never arrived, we have made the output produced by that committee available here. https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/PY-Arena-Committee-Concerns-and-Solutions-Brainstorm.pdf
