[![book](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/04/book.jpg?w=100)](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/04/book.jpg)Congratulations to [Washington Post staff writer](http://www.washingtonpost.com/pb/brigid-schulte) and Del Ray resident Brigid Schulte on the publication of her book [OVERWHELMED](http://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/review-overwhelmed-work-love-and-play-when-no-one-has-the-time-by-brigid-schulte/2014/03/21/f5649324-9e6f-11e3-9ba6-800d1192d08b_story.html): Work, Love, and Play When No One Has the Time. The book hit number 10 on the New York Times bestseller list on Sunday, March 30, but didn’t appear on the list in today’s newspaper. Perhaps potential readers were just a bit too busy last week to buy the book.
