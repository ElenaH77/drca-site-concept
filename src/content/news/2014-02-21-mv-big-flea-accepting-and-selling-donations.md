[![big-flea-logo2](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/02/big-flea-logo2.jpg)](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/02/big-flea-logo2.jpg)The [MV Big Flea](http://mvbigflea.wordpress.com/), a giant flea market that serves as the major fundraiser for the Mount Vernon Community School, will be accepting as well as selling donations on Saturday, February 22, between 2 and 4 p.m., at 3815 Mount Vernon Ave. (in the same shopping center as MOMS Organic Market). If you can’t deliver donations in person, you can email mvbigfleadonations@gmail.com for a pickup. A list of acceptable items for donation can be [found here](http://mvbigflea.wordpress.com/).
