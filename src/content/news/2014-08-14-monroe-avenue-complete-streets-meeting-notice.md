[![E Monroe Existing - StreetMix](http://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/08/e-monroe-existing-streetmix.png?w=150)](https://delraycitizen.files.wordpress.com/2014/08/e-monroe-existing-streetmix.png)The second community meeting for the [Monroe Avenue Complete Streets project ](http://alexandriava.gov/localmotion/info/default.aspx?id=74369)will be held on September 4th at 6pm at Commonwealth Academy (1321 Leslie Ave). The purpose of this meeting will be to review recommendations for the Monroe Avenue corridor and to gather community feedback on the proposals. For additional meeting details, please view the [City Calendar](http://apps.alexandriava.gov/Calendar/Detail.aspx?si=8788).
