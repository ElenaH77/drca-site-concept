    * **Stormwater Fee Presentation,** Jesse Maines, Rachel Moxley, Danilo Nuñez, City of Alexandria, Transportation and Environmental Services 
      * What is fee? 
        * Based on amount of impervious surface on property
        * Runoff goes in storm water system —> local waterways after picking up pollutants
        * Council initially allocated 0.5 cent of real tax; now replaced by fee. EPA started enforcement
        * As a fee brings in non-profits and church properties
        * Residential fee is based on hosing type 
          * Condos
          * Townhomes
          * SFH
          * Large SFH
        * Non-residential different rates
        * Utility viewer to predict fee
        * Billing starts in May 2018, 2 payments a year
        * Credit system to reduce fee 
          * Manual for ideas (expected Fall 2018)
          * Examples include rain barrels and use of permeable surfaces
        * Questions 
          * Assistance for older citizen? Not yet
          * Year 1 reductions? No.Credits can apply in second year
          * More information at Beattley Library Open House, January 29
