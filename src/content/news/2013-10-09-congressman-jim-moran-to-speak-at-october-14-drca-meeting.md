The [DRCA membership meeting](http://delraycitizen.wordpress.com/meetings/ "Meetings / Events") on Monday, October 14 will feature a talk by [U.S. Congressman Jim Moran](http://moran.house.gov/), pending, of course, the need for him to be on the Hill for votes on ending the current government shutdown and increasing the federal debt limit. The meeting will begin at 7:30 p.m., with refreshments at 7 p.m. Meeting location: Reingold building, 433 E. Monroe Ave.
