On the evening of Friday, November 19, 2021, a two-story duplex in Del Ray, Alexandria caught fire. The three-alarm fire caused extensive damage to the entire duplex, displacing two families. The Del Ray Citizens Association [raised over $38,000](https://www.gofundme.com/f/DelRayFireVictims "https://www.gofundme.com/f/DelRayFireVictims") to help the victims of the fire in the spirit of supporting our neighbors as they begin to recover and rebuild. Thank you to those that donated!
