New for the fall of 2021 - Del Ray Citizens Association t-shirts! Bonus - these shirts will double as a fundraiser to help make up the losses from not being able to hold the chili cookoff last year. The campaign runs through Tuesday, November 2 - shirts will cost $18 each and are available in four colors. Youth sizes are also available!

View our [campaign page](https://www.customink.com/fundraising/del-ray-community-association-t-shirt-sale-0644?side=front&type=1&zoom=false) for more information and to order. Also like and share this with your friends and neighbors! Shirts are expected to arrive in mid-November. Expect distribution details in the next newsletter. For a small additional fee you can also have your shirts shipped directly to you.

![gold t-shirt with "Del Ray Citizens Association Established 1954"](https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/DRCA-2021-tshirt_gold_front-300x287.jpg)![gold t-shirt with DRCA logo](https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/DRCA-2021-tshirt_gold_back-300x287.jpg)

Please note that stock has been hit hard by the COVID-19 pandemic and some colors may experience a delay in shipping that won't be known until the campaign closes and order is placed in November. We tried picking colors without these limitations but recognize things might change between now and November when the order is placed.

To learn more, or place an order, visit our Custom Ink campaign page: [https://www.customink.com/fundraising/del-ray-community-association-t-shirt-sale-0644?side=front&type=1&zoom=false](https://www.customink.com/fundraising/del-ray-community-association-t-shirt-sale-0644?side=front&type=1&zoom=false).
