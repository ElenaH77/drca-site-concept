**Help DRCA advocate for you!**

Thanks to those of you who completed the City of Alexandria's brief survey in December as part of their initial data collection to inform the redevelopment plans for Mount Vernon Avenue area ([information available on city website](https://www.alexandriava.gov/planning/info/default.aspx?id=110833)). As discussed at the [December 2019 meeting](https://delraycitizens.org/2020/01/membership-meeting-minutes-december-11-2019/), this will be the first major update since the creation of the [2005 Mount Vernon Avenue Business Plan](https://www.alexandriava.gov/uploadedFiles/planning/info/masterplan/City_Master_Plan_Map/MountVernonAvenueBusinessAreaPlanCurrent.pdf) and the [2003 Long-term Vision and Action Plan for the Arlandria neighborhood](https://www.alexandriava.gov/uploadedFiles/planning/info/masterplan/City_Master_Plan_Map/ArlandriaNeighborhoodActionPlanCurrent.pdf).

To ensure that the DRCA is advocating for the requests of our community, we're hoping we can get five more minutes of your time to provide us with some feedback on the amenities residents would like preserved or improved in the new plan.

Please take a moment to complete this**[brief survey](https://survey.sogosurvey.com/survey.aspx?k=SsQVRVVTsRsPsPsP&lang=0&data=FQVqR5hZ%2bBQ7oBc%3d)**. (Note: This survey is separate from the one the city sent out and is strictly to assist DRCA’s advocacy to the City.)
