The links below will direct you to a new website unaffiliated with the Del Ray Citizens website. DRCA does not officially endorse or guarantee the content of these external sites. Please report any dead links to admin@delraycitizen.net. 

**Del Ray Sites**

  * [Art on the Avenue](http://www.artontheavenue.org/) \- a great neighborhood event every October
  * [Del Ray Artisans](http://www.thedelrayartisans.org/) \- regular art exhibits and much more!
  * [DIY Del Ray](http://diydelray.com/) \- ideas for home design, entertaining and much more
  * Del Ray's own [Irish Breakfast Band](http://www.irishbreakfastband.com/)
  * [Del Ray Business Association](http://www.visitdelray.com/)[ ](http://www.delraycitizen.org/editor_upload/File/Misc%20Documents/DRBA_MapFlyer.pdf)
  * [Del Ray Patch](http://delray.patch.com/) \- Local coverage of Del Ray news.
  * [Friends of the Washington and Old Dominion Trail](http://www.wodfriends.org/) \-- The trail doesn't run through our neighborhood, but the W&OD Railroad was a major part of Del Ray's history.
  * [Friends of the Duncan Library](http://friendsofduncanlibrary.org/) \-- Help support our neighborhood public library.
  * [Warwick Village Citizens Association](http://www.warwickvillage.com/)

**Community Web Groups and Blogs**

  * [ACTion Alexandria](http://www.actionalexandria.org/) – where neighbors and organizations connect to pitch in and build a better community
  * [DRCA's Yahoo Group](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/delraycitizens/) \-- Our old Yahoo Group is an open membership group that is used as a public forum for Del Ray Residents. Go to the FAQ link on the left side on the web page for more info.
  * [Del Ray Parents Yahoo Group](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/delrayparents/) \-- The "Del Ray Parents" group was created so that parents in the neighborhood (and other close-by neighborhoods) can share information, resources and sometimes get to know each other to improve the time quality of Del Ray families.
  * The [Nelson Avenue Gang](http://nelsonavegang.com/) blog
  * [The LesDunBelle Diary](http://lesdunbelle.wordpress.com/) \- A cozy blog for the neighborhood composed of the intersection of Leslie, Duncan, and Bellefonte Avenues.
  * The [MV Big Flea](http://mvbigflea.wordpress.com/) \-- An annual flea market fundraiser for the Mount Vernon Community School conducted by the PTA
  * [GWSleptHere.com](http://www.gwslepthere.com/) \- Michael Bergin's community centric real estate blog
  * [Eco-City Alexandria Blog](http://ecocityalexandria.wordpress.com/) \- a place where all members of the Alexandria community can share ideas about how to make the Alexandria an ecologically sustainable city.
  * [The Arlandrian](http://www.arlandria.org/) \- a blog about and serving Arlandria
  * [News from the Avenue](https://www.facebook.com/NewsfromtheAvenue) Facebook page
