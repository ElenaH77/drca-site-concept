### It's time to renew Your DRCA Membership for 2023-2024

The DRCA Membership year runs from October 1 - September 30, so now is the time to make sure your membership is renewed for the upcoming year.

  * You can [renew your membership online](https://drca.wildapricot.org/join-us) or by mailing a check to PO Box 2233 Alexandria VA 22301
  * Not sure if you're a member? Contact membership@delraycitizen.net to find out.
  * Want to become a member? Memberships are only $25/year, and September is the perfect time to join and get the most out of your membership. [Click here to join.](https://drca.wildapricot.org/join-us)
