DRCA is raising money to help support the Del Ray Gateway Project which will convert the existing (permanently closed) Colasonta Pool into a splash park and the Nancy Dunning Memorial Garden. The site is located at the intersection of Mt. Vernon Ave and Commonwealth Ave at the North end of Del Ray. Funds from the 2016 Chili Cook Off and the 2018 House and Garden Tour will be donated to the fundraising efforts. Learn more at <https://www.facebook.com/delraygateway/>
