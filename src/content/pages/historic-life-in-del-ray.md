This page is dedicated to collecting historic resources about life in the Town of Potomac and early Del Ray.   [**Del Ray and the Town of Potomac History from the City of Alexandria**](https://www.alexandriava.gov/historic-sites/history-of-del-ray-and-the-town-of-potomac) Includes details on all of the Heritage Signs around Del Ray, as well as Oral History interviews with a number of long time Del Ray residents. [**Town of Potomac Historical Association**](https://www.potomacva.org/) A living history of the Town of Potomac. The Historical Association also administers our [Plaque Program](https://delraycitizens.org/history/plaque-program/).   **Interesting Del Ray History Reads:** [The Hidden History of the St. Asaph Racetrack](https://www.connectionnewspapers.com/news/2012/aug/23/hidden-history-st-asaph-racetrack/) [The Hidden History of Del Ray](https://www.alexandriagazette.com/news/2019/mar/22/hidden-history-del-ray/)   **Research Resources:** [Archives of Virginia Newspapers](https://virginiachronicle.com/?) (including the [Alexandria Gazette](https://virginiachronicle.com/?a=cl&cl=CL1&sp=AG&e=-------en-20--1--txt-txIN--------)) Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps of Alexandria (Via the Library of Congress): Del Ray is depicted in [1921 ](https://www.loc.gov/item/sanborn08968_007/)and [1941](https://www.loc.gov/item/sanborn08968_008/) Census Documents covering Del Ray: [1910](https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/1910-Del-Ray-Census.pdf), [1920](https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/1920-Del-Ray-Area-Census.pdf), and [1930](https://delraycitizens.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/1930-Census-Alexandria-District-2.pdf) [A History of Racially Restrictive Real Estate Covenants in Alexandria](https://www.alexandriava.gov/sites/default/files/2023-03/Z4H-Event-Moon-Presentation-Covenants-Land-Use-March2023.pdf) (includes an image of the 1924 Town of Potomac Yearbook). An interactive map of racially restrictive covenants by address can be found at [https://documentingexclusion.org/map/](https://documentingexclusion.org/map/)
