About the DRCA

A civic association, since 1954.

The Del Ray Citizens Association (DRCA) is a non-profit 501(c)(4) organization for Del Ray residents and business owners in Alexandria, Virginia. We keep members informed on land use, public safety, schools, parks and recreation, transportation, housing, and the other issues that shape quality of life — and we represent the neighborhood before the Planning Commission, Board of Zoning Appeals, and City Council.

The DRCA meets at 7:00 pm on the second Wednesday of the month, September through June, typically via Zoom. Meeting details are shared in the member newsletter.

Why join?

Stay informed

Monthly meetings and newsletters cover land use, safety, transportation, volunteering, and events before they hit your feed.

Advocate

The DRCA carries member positions into city forums — one letter from a thousand households lands differently than one from one.

Vote

Members debate and decide DRCA positions — from restaurant patio expansions to zoning changes next to your alley.

Community

The feel of Del Ray doesn't maintain itself. Dues fund the events that bring neighbors together.

The Del Ray House & Garden Tour

A self-guided walking tour of Del Ray's most interesting homes, ADUs, and gardens — held every other year on the Saturday of Mother's Day weekend since 1999.

About the tour

The Tour is DRCA's largest fundraiser, and proceeds from ticket sales stay in our community. The 2026 tour featured twelve homes and gardens and supported the Scholarship Fund of Alexandria, after-school tutoring at Mount Vernon Community School, and restoration of the Del Ray Little Red Caboose.

Previous tours have supported scholarships for local students, the Duncan Library, the Nancy Dunning Memorial Garden, the Del Ray Gateway Project, the Mt. Vernon School playground, and neighborhood parks.
